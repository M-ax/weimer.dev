Industrial software earns trust slowly. The people using it have a mental model of the process, the equipment, and the cost of an unclear action. A useful interface should meet them there.

In semiconductor manufacturing, details such as material state, equipment capability, and protocol expectations are not edge cases. They are the shape of the problem. Standards from SEMI help systems agree on those details, but thoughtful software is what makes the agreement usable.

That is why I like to begin with the workflow before the screen: what is true now, what may change next, and what information helps someone make a confident decision? The code gets better once those questions have clear answers.
